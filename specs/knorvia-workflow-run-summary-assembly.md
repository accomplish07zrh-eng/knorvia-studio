## Design and preserved behavior

One synchronous summary operation evaluates an ordered required/optional sentence program. A length-accounting prefix fitter owns the 400 UTF-16-unit budget: discard whole optional sentences from the end, retain both required sentences, then hard-slice with the original one-unit ellipsis when necessary. This replaces repeated joins/pops and array concatenation, not the clause algorithms. No await, state, IO, cache, retry, permission, deadline or completion owner is introduced. All existing clause helpers, public declaration and inherited comments/prose remain byte-identical. Compatibility expressions (template interpolation, undefined-only omission, separators and UTF-16 slicing) remain explicit retained material; this is not a whole-file originality or licence determination.

Read order: generatedAt; terminal status; status+phase; steps; failure; questions; progress; deliverable; ownership. Every optional reader is evaluated before fitting, even when its result will be dropped. Getters, native array methods/receivers, repeated nested reads, coercion failures and exceptions preserve order and identity. No validation or normalization is added to malformed facts. Phase indices/plurals, live roster breakdown, leftover terminal steps, unknown questions, stalled age, primary artifact selection and cross-session prose stay exact. UTF-16 hard slicing may split a surrogate pair, as in the baseline.

The actual consumer remains GetWorkflowRun's synchronous summary call after its sole journal-port await and fact projection. Registry gates, schema identity, 10-second deadline, permission and declared cancellation contract remain exact. External abort races are observed through the unchanged deadline owner/full call-runner; current output, result/error events, metadata and Completed/Cancelled telemetry must match the exact baseline. No extra promise settlement is permitted.
